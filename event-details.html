Case Study Analysis

Global Enterprise Leadership Summit

Location Geneva, Switzerland
Delegates 2,400 Executives
Duration 3 Days (4 Tracks)
Service Scope Turnkey AV & Ops

The Brief & Event Objectives

Our enterprise client required a high-capacity international summit bringing together C-suite executives from 48 countries. The primary challenge: coordinating 65 keynote speakers across 4 simultaneous hall tracks while executing ultra-low-latency 4K global broadcast feeds to 14,000 remote shareholders.

Production & Operational Strategy

Summit & Co. deployed a 45-person on-site operations team. We constructed a custom 24-meter ultra-curved LED mainstage backdrop with dynamic acoustic baffles, implemented redundant gigabit fiber distribution, and oversaw VIP diplomatic escort protocol.
